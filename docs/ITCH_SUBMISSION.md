## Description

It’s 17:59. Everybody else has gone home.

There are three valves still hissing in the boiler room. Gary forgot his lunch again. And somebody ought to water those plants on the roof.

You can leave a place a little better than you found it. Even when nobody is watching.

LAST LIGHT is a short pixel puzzle platformer about the last shift of the day. Press Shift to change the building: gold machinery works during the day, blue shadow bridges appear at night. Jump between both rhythms, finish three small tasks on each floor, and take the lift toward home.

Move with A/D or arrow keys. Jump with Space, W or up. Shift or X changes day/night. E uses the lift. M mutes; Esc pauses. Touch buttons are available on touch devices. English and Chinese story text. Progress saves locally, and falling never erases completed kindnesses.

The artwork is drawn in code with a shared pixel palette. All music, ambience and sound effects are synthesized with WebAudio. Day and night share a melody with different arrangements. Headphones recommended.

Made for GitHub Game Off 2026 — SHIFT. AI was used to assist with code, procedural art, synthesized audio, writing and testing. No recorded voices or external samples. Fictional story.
